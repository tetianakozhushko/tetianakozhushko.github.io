LumiBook

A mobile concept for discovering beauty services, comparing specialists and booking a salon appointment.

The project

Booking a salon visit means comparing the service, specialist, time and price. I explored an app that makes those choices visible before someone confirms.

My role

I developed LumiBook independently during my internship. I mapped the journey, explored interface directions and designed the booking flow and visual system.

The concept has yet to be tested with clients.

View on Behance ↗

Deciding what to book

The brief, proto-persona and journey map identify the decisions someone makes from discovering a service to choosing a specialist.

From browsing to confirmation

I explored how service details, reviews, prices and availability could support the same short booking flow.

The interface system

I kept the palette warm and the layouts quiet so photos, prices and available times were easy to compare.
