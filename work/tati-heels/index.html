Tati Heels

A live Telegram Mini App I designed and built for my dance classes in Warsaw, with scheduling, reservations, pass tracking and payment status.

The project

I teach High Heels classes in Warsaw. My students needed a place to check the schedule, reserve a spot and see their remaining classes in a pass. I built that into Telegram, where they already reach me.

My role

I designed and built the Mini App using AI-assisted coding. I maintain it alongside teaching and use the day-to-day booking routine to decide what to improve.

View on Behance ↗

The routine behind the app

The case follows eight steps, from opening the schedule to coming back for another class.

From schedule to studio

Students can filter the schedule, see what they need to know before booking, and find their confirmation, reminder and studio directions in the same place.

Payments, passes and repeat visits

Payment instructions and pass balances answer the questions that used to come up in messages. The case also shows how the app handles demand for a returning class.
