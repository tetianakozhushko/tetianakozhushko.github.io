GRC Trail

Product design for a B2B compliance workspace, from guided setup and risk assessment to evidence collection and a public Trust Center.

The project

Compliance teams have to decide what to do next, who owns it, and what evidence will stand up in an audit. GRC Trail puts those decisions in a single workspace for teams working with GDPR, SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

My role

As the sole product designer, I owned the information architecture, user flows, interface design and Figma components. I worked with developers on implementation.

View on Behance ↗

The compliance journey

A six-stage model gives teams a way to see where they are and what needs attention next.

From setup to ownership

I designed guided setup with Sherpa, risk views that put the assessment in plain language, and controls with a named owner.

Evidence and the Trust Center

Evidence collection and the public Trust Center sit at opposite ends of the same workflow. The interface uses a shared set of components and reserves color for status.
