What About Tours

Post-booking design for a travel marketplace, including trip details, meeting points, cancellations and reviews on desktop and mobile.

The project

Once a tour is booked, travelers still need to find the meeting point, understand changes and know what to do if a plan falls through. This case covers the part of the marketplace they use after checkout.

My role

I designed the traveler flows and responsive screens as part of a team. My scope included client feedback, developer handoff, mobile adaptations and selected operator screens.

View on Behance ↗

The brief and my scope

A real client project with separate traveler and operator experiences. My main area was the traveler journey after purchase.

The booking and the day of the tour

I designed the booking list, its detail page and the day-of-tour view so travelers could reach the right information as the trip approached.

Exceptions and follow-up

I mapped the states around cancellations and payment, adapted the key flows for mobile and covered what happens after a tour, from reviews to messages.

Feedback and developer handoff

The client’s notes and the annotated flows show how the screens were revised and prepared for development. The case also identifies the operator screens I contributed to.
