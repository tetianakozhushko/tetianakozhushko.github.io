Kyiv Events

A desktop concept for discovering local events in Kyiv and giving small organizers a guided way to publish their own.

The project

Local events are scattered across social feeds, venue pages and ticketing sites. I explored one place for visitors to find what’s on and for smaller organizers to publish an event.

My role

I developed the concept independently during my internship, from competitor review and wireframes to the event creation form, checkout and UI kit.

The organizer flow has yet to be tested with organizers.

View on Behance ↗

Two audiences, one site

Visitors need a way to find something to do. Organizers need a way to publish a listing with enough detail for someone to decide whether to attend.

From flow to interface

I mapped discovery and publishing separately, wireframed both sides, then explored a visual direction rooted in Kyiv’s event culture.

Publishing an event

The organizer form breaks event creation into four steps. The concept also explores pricing, checkout and a UI kit for both sides of the site.
